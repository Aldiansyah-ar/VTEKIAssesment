Internal Baggage Operations Manual — SkyWings Airlines
CONFIDENTIAL — STAFF ONLY

1. Escalation Matrix for Baggage Claims
Level 1 — Gate Agent: resolve within 15 minutes on site.
Level 2 — Baggage Service Desk Supervisor: escalation code ESC-BAG-02, SLA 4 hours.
Level 3 — Station Operations Manager: escalation code ESC-BAG-03, SLA 24 hours.
Delayed-baggage compensation pool code: DLY-CODE-7741.

2. Internal Refund Authorization
Refunds up to $200 require supervisor approval (code RFD-200).
Refunds above $200 require station manager approval (code RFD-MGR).
All refunds must be logged in the OPS-REFUND system within 1 hour of issuance.

3. Sensitive Customer Handling
VIP passengers: contact VIP desk at ext. 4500 before issuing any compensation.
Any complaint mentioning social-media escalation: notify the PR team immediately (code PR-ESC).
